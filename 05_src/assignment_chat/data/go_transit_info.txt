GO Transit Knowledge Base

1. GO Transit
GO Transit is a regional public transportation system
serving the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area and
surrounding regions in Ontario, Canada.

2. GO Trains
GO Transit operates regional train services connecting
Toronto with surrounding communities.

3. Union Station
Union Station in downtown Toronto is a major
transportation hub for GO Transit.

4. PRESTO
PRESTO is an electronic fare payment system used
on GO Transit and other participating transit systems.

5. GO Transit Information
Passengers can use official GO Transit information
to check routes, schedules, fares, and service updates.

6. Accessibility
GO Transit provides accessibility information and
services to help passengers plan their journeys.

7. Bicycles
GO Transit has rules about bringing bicycles
on trains. Passengers should check the current
official bicycle policy before travelling.
