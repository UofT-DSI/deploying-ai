## Workplace readiness
The public Google AI Essentials outline presents foundational AI skills for people without prior technical experience. Its themes include choosing AI tools, working with prompts, improving productivity, using AI responsibly, and continuing to learn as tools evolve. This file is an original synthesis from that public overview, not a transcription of course material.

## Adoption approach
Start with tasks people understand, make the expected benefits explicit, and create time for practice. Staff should know when an AI tool is permitted, when human judgment takes priority, and where to ask for help. Managers should encourage feedback about confusing workflows and unexpected errors.

## Team-level actions
Identify one use case per team; clarify what data may be used; offer a short demonstration and hands-on exercise; identify a point of contact; and review results after a pilot. Avoid requiring employees to adopt new tools without explaining purpose, expectations, and safeguards.

## Questions for Alva
- Which role-specific tasks could benefit from support rather than full automation?
- Do employees have access, time, and permission to experiment safely?
- What feedback or training will be needed before wider adoption?

This is general learning guidance rather than an assessment of the effectiveness of any particular AI product.
