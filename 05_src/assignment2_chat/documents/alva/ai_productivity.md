## What the public curriculum covers
Google AI Essentials introduces practical ways to use generative AI to support everyday productivity, develop ideas, draft materials, and make informed workplace choices. The publicly available course overview emphasizes hands-on practice, prompts, responsible use, and selecting tools for work tasks. This document is an independently written learning summary, not a reproduction of paid course lessons.

## Example applications
For routine communications, AI can help draft a first pass that an employee edits for accuracy, tone, and organizational requirements. For planning, it can propose outline options or potential questions that the team evaluates. For an information-heavy task, it may help organize provided notes, but staff should verify outputs against the original records.

## Measuring benefit
Choose a task with a clear baseline and ask whether AI reduces time without harming quality. Track rework, error rates, and employee confidence, not simply the number of prompts used. If verification takes more time than the task saves, consider changing the workflow.

## Suggested exercise
Choose a low-risk recurring task. Perform it manually, then with an approved AI tool. Compare time, completeness, and accuracy. Record what human editing was required.
