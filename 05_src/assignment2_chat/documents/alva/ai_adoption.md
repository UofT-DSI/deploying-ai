# AI Adoption and Organizational Change

**Knowledge base:** Alva — AI Workforce Readiness Advisor  
**Primary source(s):** Prosci and Microsoft Learn  
**Document type:** Independently written educational synthesis (not official course text)  
**Compiled:** 2026-11-17

## AI adoption is both technical and human
Adopting an AI tool involves more than granting access. Employees need to understand the purpose, trust the safeguards, learn usable skills, and incorporate new practices into their jobs. Prosci emphasizes the people side of adoption, while Microsoft Learn highlights responsible use, protecting sensitive information, and checking AI-generated results.

## Common barriers
Potential obstacles include unclear leadership expectations, limited knowledge, fear of role changes, insufficient hands-on experience, and uncertainty about privacy or accuracy. The appropriate response depends on the barrier; a generic training course will not necessarily solve lack of trust or ambiguous policy.

## A small pilot
Select one defined use case and a small affected group. Explain why the work is changing and what success looks like. Provide guidance and time to practice. Document errors and questions. Review quality, willingness to use the tool, and ongoing support needs before expansion.

## Questions for Alva
- What does the team expect AI to improve?
- Which specific tasks, roles, or decisions will change?
- Is the barrier awareness, willingness, knowledge, practical ability, or reinforcement?
- How will managers distinguish genuine uptake from superficial usage metrics?

## Boundary
No general model can guarantee adoption; leaders must investigate their own organizational context.
