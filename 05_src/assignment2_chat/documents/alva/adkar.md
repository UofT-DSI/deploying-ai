## The model
Prosci's ADKAR model describes five elements required for successful individual change: Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, and Reinforcement. It is a model for individual transitions; Prosci distinguishes it from its broader organizational change management methodology.

## Applying ADKAR to workplace AI
Awareness: People understand why the organization is introducing an AI tool. Desire: They see reasons to engage and have a chance to voice concerns. Knowledge: They learn what the tool does, what the policy allows, and how to work with it. Ability: They can apply the skills in realistic work tasks with support. Reinforcement: Feedback, coaching, and routines help sustain appropriate use.

## Diagnostic questions
- Awareness: Can employees explain why this change is happening?
- Desire: What benefits, concerns, or incentives influence participation?
- Knowledge: Do employees know the steps and safe-use rules?
- Ability: Can they successfully perform a relevant task in practice?
- Reinforcement: What support or recognition will sustain change?

## Example
If staff understand the reason for AI adoption but cannot use the tool reliably, another awareness campaign alone will not address the problem. A practical exercise and coaching may be more appropriate. If staff do not trust the purpose of the change, technical training by itself may also fall short.
