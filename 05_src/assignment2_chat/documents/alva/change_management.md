## Organizational change
Change management supports people as they adopt new ways of working. Prosci presents an organizational methodology with phases to prepare an approach, manage change, and sustain outcomes, alongside the individual-level ADKAR model. Technical deployment and employee adoption require related but distinct attention.

## What leaders can do
Define success in terms of work practices and outcomes, not only the availability of software. Identify affected groups and their concerns, involve sponsors and people managers, and prepare tailored communications and support. Pilot the change with feedback loops, then assess whether new behaviors continue after launch.

## AI-specific considerations
Introducing AI may alter tasks, responsibilities, decision rights, and employee confidence. Build opportunities for people to ask questions about data use, job implications, review responsibilities, and tool limitations. Practical role-specific training can be more useful than generic announcements.

## Evaluation questions
- Are sponsors aligned on why the tool is needed?
- Which teams face the largest workflow changes?
- Can employees demonstrate safe and useful adoption?
- What barriers are appearing, and who can resolve them?

## Suggested next step
Create a small stakeholder map and identify one measurable adoption behavior before planning communication or training.
